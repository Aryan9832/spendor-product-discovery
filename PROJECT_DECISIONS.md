# Project decisions agreed with Spendor

- **Brand and assets:** Spendor has authorized use of the current website's product information, exact product imagery, name, and brand assets for this private interview portfolio project.
- **Catalog scope:** Include products currently present on the public Spendor website. Product specification PDFs are the reference source for structured product facts.
- **Recommendation policy:** The prototype may derive preliminary recommendations from published product dimensions, type, amplifier range, frequency response, and placement information. It must not present subjective listening claims as fact without a source or review.
- **Review owner:** The GM (Aryan's dad) is the business reviewer for product accuracy and approved presentation.
- **Publication boundary:** This is private and intended to demonstrate skills during interviews; it is not a public Spendor storefront or an official customer-facing launch.
- **Excluded data:** No customer data, internal pricing, sales data, credentials, or unreleased product information is used.
